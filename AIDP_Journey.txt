My AIDP Journey: Diagnosis, Treatment, and Potential Root Cause
后半部分是中文版.
Background, Dental Antecedent, & Initial Symptoms (July – Early August)
•	Around July 20: Had a mild toothache.
•	July 23: Visited a general dental office and was referred to a root canal specialist. A subsequent CT scan revealed that the 18th tooth was broken. Due to a poor prognosis for a root canal, extraction was recommended.
•	August 6: Underwent oral surgery to have the broken 18th tooth extracted. Note: This timeline is highly correlated with the subsequent neurological symptoms, serving as a potential autoimmune trigger for the AIDP.
•	Late July: Began experiencing mild sensory and motor deficits, including light numbness, tingling, muscle weakness, and gait imbalance in peripheral extremities (fingers and toes).
•	August 9–14: Neurological symptoms progressed rapidly in a bottom-up pattern, accompanied by acute autonomic dysfunction (severe urinary retention and an absolute lack of bowel movements for six consecutive days from Aug 9 to Aug 14).
Detailed Timeline
Monday, August 10
•	Maintained normal functioning most of the day but suffered a sudden fall at home while attempting to place a watermelon on the floor.
Tuesday, August 11
•	Contacted multiple general neurology practices; discovered that new patient appointments were unavailable until October or November.
•	Scheduled an appointment with the Georgia Epilepsy & Neurology Institute for August 14 based on a Google AI recommendation.
•	Fine motor control deteriorated; unable to use chopsticks for dinner.
•	Suffered a second fall outside while walking to take a night shower.
Wednesday, August 12
•	Suffered a third fall around 3:00 PM in the way to restroom at the Fiserv Alpharetta office.
•	Fine motor deficits persisted; remained unable to use chopsticks for dinner.
Thursday, August 13 (Hospital Admission)
•	Morning: Suffered a fourth fall from the garage deck down to the garage floor. Arrived at work and suffered a fifth fall on the second floor of the indoor Fiserv parking lot. Walking from the parking lot to the office building became exceptionally difficult. Front desk personnel provided a wheelchair and escorted me to the Wellness Center.  A colleague, Leon, drove me home, and my wife immediately drove me to the Emergency Room at Emory Johns Creek Hospital, where I was admitted. Cancelled the appointment with the Georgia Epilepsy & Neurology Institute.
•	Diagnosis & Initial Treatment: Diagnosed with Acute Inflammatory Demyelinating Polyneuropathy (AIDP), which was definitively confirmed via a lumbar puncture (spinal tap). The condition involves the immune system being provoked into producing antibodies that attack the peripheral nerves. Intravenous Immunoglobulin (IVIG) therapy—the preferred non-invasive, first-line treatment to neutralize harmful antibodies—was initiated immediately with the first daily dose.
Friday, August 14 (ICU Transfer)
•	Experienced profound urinary retention; required a urinary catheter. A stool cleanup procedure was also administered.
•	Following the second dose of IVIG, symptoms temporarily worsened. Developed new cranial nerve symptoms mimicking a mild stroke, including numbness around the mouth, a slight left-sided lip droop, and mild swallowing difficulties (dysphagia).
•	Transferred to the Intensive Care Unit (ICU) for closer observation. The swallowing difficulty was objectively confirmed via a video fluoroscopic swallowing exam (X-ray) a few days later.
Saturday, August 15
•	Perioral (mouth) numbness became more serious following the third dose of IVIG.  Had nausea and vomiting after IVIG.
•	Treatment Modification: The medical team decided to discontinue IVIG due to symptom progression and pivoted to Plasmapheresis (Plasma Exchange) to physically remove the harmful antibodies from the blood.
•	Transferred out of the ICU back to a standard floor room at my request to allow for better rest, as hourly ICU blood pressure monitoring was severely disrupting my sleep.
Sunday, August 16
•	Spontaneous urinary function returned. Neurological symptoms stabilized with no significant changes. An MRI was performed around this time.
Monday, August 17
•	Morning: Underwent a minor surgical procedure to insert a central venous catheter (Y-tube) into my jugular neck vein to establish vascular access for apheresis.
•	Late Afternoon: Completed the first plasma exchange session, lasting approximately 130 minutes.
Tuesday, August 18
•	Rest day between treatments. Noted a clear improvement in mouth symptoms and had a normal bowel movement.
Wednesday, August 19
•	Morning: Completed the second plasma exchange session.
•	Following this session, the facial numbness completely resolved, lip muscle function returned to normal, and the swallowing difficulties vanished.
Friday, August 21
•	Morning: Completed the third plasma exchange session.
Monday, August 24
•	Completed the fourth plasma exchange session.
Wednesday, August 26 (Discharge)
•	Morning: Completed the fifth and final plasma exchange session.
•	4:00 PM: Officially discharged from the hospital.
Current Status and Outlook
At the time of discharge, my physical mobility and neurological baseline had recovered to roughly where they were on the afternoon of August 12. While the day-to-day progress is very incremental and slow, I am experiencing continuous, steady improvement. Full nerve regeneration and recovery from AIDP is a long-term journey that will require significant time and patience.  Fortunately, my management team allows me to work from home during my recovery period.
 

我的 AIDP 诊疗历程：诊断、治疗及潜在病因
背景、前期牙科病史及初期症状（7月 – 8月初）
•	7月20日前后： 开始出现轻微的牙痛。
•	7月23日： 就诊于普通牙科诊所，随后被转诊给牙髓病专科医生（根管治疗专家）。CT 检查显示第18颗牙齿已经折裂。由于做根管治疗的预后较差，医生建议拔除。
•	8月6日： 接受口腔手术，拔除了折裂的第18颗牙齿。
•	注： 牙痛这一时间线与随后出现的神经系统症状高度吻合，可能是引发 AIDP 的自身免疫触发点。
•	7月下旬： 末梢肢体开始出现轻微的感觉和运动障碍，表现为手指和脚趾的轻微麻木、刺痛、肌肉无力以及行走不平衡。
•	8月9日–14日： 神经系统症状呈“自下而上”的趋势迅速恶化，并伴有严重的自主神经功能障碍（表现为急性尿潴留，且在8月9日至8月14日的连续六天内完全没有排便）。
详细时间线
8月10日，星期一
•	大部分时间状态正常，但在家中试图将西瓜放到地上时，身体突然失去平衡摔倒。
8月11日，星期二
•	联系了多家普通神经内科诊所；发现新患者的预约已经排到了10月甚至11月。
•	根据谷歌 AI 的推荐，预约了 佐治亚癫痫与神经学研究所（Georgia Epilepsy & Neurology Institute） 8月14日的号。
•	手部精细动作控制能力进一步恶化；晚餐时已无法使用筷子。
•	夜间去洗澡时再次摔倒。
8月12日，星期三
•	下午3点左右，在 Fiserv Alpharetta 办公室 去洗手间第三次摔倒。
•	手部精细动作障碍持续，晚餐时依然无法使用筷子。
8月13日，星期四（入院当天）
•	上午： 在家中从车库露台摔落到车库地面（第四次摔倒）。 抵达公司后，在 Fiserv 室内停车场二楼摔倒（第五次摔倒）。从停车场走到办公楼异常艰难。前台工作人员推来轮椅将我送至健康中心（Wellness Center）。同事 Leon 开车将我送回家，随后我妻子立即开车将我送往 埃默里约翰斯克里克医院（Emory Johns Creek Hospital） 的急诊室并办理了入院。同时取消了此前预约的佐治亚癫痫与神经学研究所的号。
•	诊断与初期治疗： 被诊断为 急性炎症性脱髓鞘性多发性神经病（AIDP），并通过腰椎穿刺（脑脊液检查）得到了确诊。该病症本质上是免疫系统受到某种刺激，从而产生抗体攻击自身的周围神经。医院立即启动了静脉注射免疫球蛋白（IVIG）疗法，并在当天进行了首次每日剂量的注射。IVIG 是中和体内有害抗体的首选无创（无伤口）一线疗法。
8月14日，星期五（转入 ICU）
•	出现严重的尿潴留，尿液积聚在膀胱内极难排出；为此使用了一次导尿管。同时实施了肠道清理程序。
•	在接受第二剂 IVIG 治疗后，症状甚至加重。引发了类似于轻微中风的脑神经症状，包括嘴部周围麻木、左侧口角轻微下垂以及轻微的吞咽困难（Dysphagia）。
•	随后被转入 重症监护室（ICU） 进行更严密的观察。几天后，通过吞咽 X 光（吞咽造影检查）客观证实了吞咽困难的存在。
8月15日，星期六
•	接受第三剂 IVIG 注射后，嘴部周围的麻木症状变得更加严重，出现恶心呕吐。
•	调整治疗方案： 鉴于症状持续进展，医疗团队决定停用 IVIG，并更改为 血浆置换（Plasma Exchange） 疗法，以便直接从血液中清除有害抗体。
•	应我个人要求转出 ICU 回到普通病房。因为 ICU 要求每小时测量一次血压，这导致我无法得到很好的休息和睡眠。
8月16日，星期日
•	自主排尿功能恢复。神经系统症状趋于稳定，未见明显的进一步恶化。大约在这期间接受了核磁共振（MRI）检查。
8月17日，星期一
•	上午： 接受了一个小手术，在颈静脉安置了 Y 型管（中心静脉导管），以便为血浆置换建立血管通路。
•	下午5点左右： 进行了第一次血浆置换，持续了大约130分钟。
8月18日，星期二
•	治疗间歇的休息日。感觉嘴部症状明显好转，并且恢复了正常排便。
8月19日，星期三
•	上午9点左右： 进行了第二次血浆置换。
•	治疗结束后，嘴部周围的麻木感完全消失，口唇肌肉功能恢复正常，吞咽困难的症状也彻底消失。
8月21日，星期五
•	上午： 进行了第三次血浆置换。
8月24日，星期一
•	进行了第四次血浆置换。
8月26日，星期三（出院当天）
•	上午： 进行了第五次也是最后一次血浆置换。
•	下午4点左右： 正式办理出院。
目前现状与展望
出院时，我的肢体活动能力和神经系统基线已基本恢复到8月12日下午的状态。虽然每天的进展非常细微和缓慢，但能够感受到身体正在持续、稳定地好转。周围神经的再生以及从 AIDP 中完全康复将是一场漫长的旅程，需要给予足够的耐心与时间。幸运的是，我的领导团队允许我康复期在家工作。
